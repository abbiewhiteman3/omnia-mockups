Just a Girl · Builder Gel, Gel-X & Nail Art

Adrienne

Hi, I'm Adrienne, the girl behind Just a Girl. It was born from my love of creativity, connection, and the belief that getting your nails done should feel like more than an appointment.

It's a little ritual, a place to slow down, yap about everything and nothing, and leave with tiny pieces of art at your fingertips. I specialize in builder gel, Gel-X, and hand-painted nail art with a style that's feminine, whimsical, a little witchy, and occasionally weird in the best way.

Most of all, Just a Girl is a space to show up exactly as you are. Bring your Pinterest board, your weird little idea, or no idea at all. We'll figure it out together. Because really, we're all just people who want really cute nails.

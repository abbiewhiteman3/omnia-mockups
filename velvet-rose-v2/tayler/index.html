Tayler

Tayler is the owner of Tays Way Esthetics, LLC, dedicated to helping clients look and feel their absolute best. She specializes in lash & brow artistry, as well as customized facial treatments designed to enhance natural beauty and support healthy, glowing skin.

She has worked in a Forbes-trained luxury resort spa, recognized with multiple awards, where she refined her skills and commitment to high-level services. Tayler creates a welcoming and empowering space where every client can feel comfortable to express their desired results, ensuring you leave feeling confident and beautiful.
